Teaching Ideas

Use these prompts to guide discussion.

  • Ask students to describe what inbound, storage, picking, packing, and outbound look like in real life.
  • Create multiple orders and see what happens when there is not enough inventory – what would Amazon’s systems do?
  • Assign roles: one student controls inbound, another controls orders, another narrates the timeline.
  • Challenge: minimise “on hand” inventory while still fulfilling all orders.
